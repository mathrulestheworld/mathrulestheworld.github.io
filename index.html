Madhusudhan Reddy Pittu

Postdoctoral Associate, Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University

madhusudhan.p@nyu.edu

New York, NY

I have been a Postdoctoral Associate at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University, since September 2025, where I work with Anupam Gupta.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University in the Algorithms, Combinatorics, and Optimization (ACO) program, advised by Anupam Gupta and, during my final year, David P. Woodruff. Prior to that, I earned my B.Tech. in Computer Science from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur.

My research interests lie broadly in Theoretical Computer Science, with a focus on Approximation Algorithms, Online Algorithms, Combinatorics, and Optimization. I am particularly interested in problems with a geometric flavor.

Research profile. I have 10 peer-reviewed publications, consisting of 8 conference papers and 2 journal articles, plus 2 additional public research preprints. Six of the eight conference papers appeared at ICORE 2026 A* venues, where A* denotes a recognized flagship conference. The other two appeared at APPROX/RANDOM, an ICORE 2026 A venue. See my complete publication record for the supporting metadata and links.

My journal publications appear in Mathematical Programming, a peer-reviewed journal of the Mathematical Optimization Society, and ACM Transactions on Algorithms. Google Scholar reported 154 citations as of August 25, 2026.
