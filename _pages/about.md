I have been a Postdoctoral Associate at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, New York University, since September 2025, where I work with [Anupam Gupta](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~anupamg/).

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University in the Algorithms, Combinatorics, and Optimization (ACO) program, advised by Anupam Gupta and, during my final year, [David P. Woodruff](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dwoodruf/). Prior to that, I earned my B.Tech. in Computer Science from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur.

My research interests lie broadly in Theoretical Computer Science, with a focus on Approximation Algorithms, Online Algorithms, Combinatorics, and Optimization. I am particularly interested in problems with a geometric flavor.

**Research profile.** I have 10 peer-reviewed publications, consisting of 8 conference papers and 2 journal articles, plus 2 additional public research preprints. Six of the eight conference papers appeared at [ICORE 2026 A* venues](https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/?by=for&page=1&search=4613&sort=arank&source=ICORE2026), where A* denotes a recognized flagship conference. The other two appeared at APPROX/RANDOM, an ICORE 2026 A venue. See my [complete publication record](/publications/) for the supporting metadata and links.

My journal publications appear in *Mathematical Programming*, a peer-reviewed journal of the [Mathematical Optimization Society](https://mathopt.org/), and *ACM Transactions on Algorithms*. [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dmo4-YMAAAAJ&hl=en) reported 154 citations as of August 25, 2026.
